# PHVS research working instructions

## User-requested GenAI process documentation (8 October 2026)

Read `research/genai_process_log.json` and `research/genai_pitch_notes.txt` when
continuing substantive PHVS diligence or preparing pitch materials. These are a
record of the research process, not independent evidence for investment claims.

At the end of substantive research/modeling turns, append a concise event to the
JSON log when there is a new hypothesis, meaningful user challenge, change of
method, source discovery, correction, validation, model change, or stopping decision.
Do not log every tool call or ordinary clarification. Group related iterations.
Preserve old entries; mark superseded conclusions rather than rewriting history.

Capture: objective; user's contribution and a short exact prompt excerpt when
available (otherwise label paraphrase); AI task/method; sources/artifact paths;
decision-relevant output snapshot; effect on long/short thesis; limitations and
bias checks actually performed; remaining work. Record the real date for new work.
For backfills, distinguish logging date from unknown event date.

Credit user-originated ideas and skepticism explicitly. Do not attribute them to
the AI. Record disconfirming findings and assistant mistakes, not just successes.
Describe AI's contribution as assisted search, synthesis, extraction, programming,
or verification as applicable; do not claim that a human could not do equivalent
analysis, invent time saved, or claim novelty without evidence.

Record exact model/version, reasoning settings, agents and usage only when known
from reliable session metadata. Otherwise say unknown. Do not launch agents,
change settings, buy data, or create recurring automation just to document work.
Do not claim multiple independent prompts or model agreement unless actually run.
Distinguish source checking, arithmetic checking, sensitivity analysis and external
validation. An independent calculation is not independent market validation.

Keep `research/genai_pitch_notes.txt` concise, using the competition's five fields:
Objective; GenAI Method & Data Source; Snapshot of GenAI Output; Interpretation &
Relevance to Thesis; Limitations & Bias Mitigation. Update when a stronger example
emerges, not on every turn. Link the log from current research state.

Do not change synced project files under `sources/`. These instructions apply to
this repository; documentation does not run automatically outside active work.
