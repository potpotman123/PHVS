Guggenheim ramp and peak-sales audit — 8 October 2026

RESULT
No clear early-shape anomaly in this bounded comparison. IR resembles Orladeyo;
XR resembles Cerdelga. This is a plausibility check, not validation of PHVS sales,
market share, the opening revenue level or eventual peak. Do not reject a broker
curve merely because it differs from one analogue or because a short is preferred.

METHOD
Reused existing screened analogues and source forecasts. Extended Cerdelga through
2018 from Sanofi and Trikafta through2022 from Vertex. Compared first subsequent
full calendar years, excluding partial launch years. This approximates commercial
age; launch months differ and PHVS within-year timing is unknown. Normalize each
series to100 in its own first full calendar year to remove starting scale/currency.
Compare growth rates as well as levels. No logistic/S-curve was statistically fit.

INTERPRETATION
XR is already more front-loaded relative to its fourth full year than Orladeyo:
year1 is41.6% of year4 versus28.0%. Thus slower percentage growth after a strong
first year is consistent with the existing model's higher opening XR revenue.
IR year1 is26.3% of year4, close to Orladeyo28.0%. This removes one proposed
critique (obviously exceptional normalized early growth), not the commercial debate.

Cerdelga is a useful later-oral rare-disease comparison but its earlier oral had
limitations, eligibility is pharmacogenetic, and Sanofi had an established franchise.
Trikafta is only a long-case context check: expanded genotypes, pediatric labels,
regional launches and an incumbent franchise confound a pure switching story.
All series are global reported revenue: access/geographic expansion, net price,
FX and stocking can move revenue without equivalent patient-adoption changes.
The sample was purposively selected and mostly successful. It cannot establish a
normal distribution, probability of success or optimal PHVS curve. No p-values or
fitted confidence interval are appropriate here. Scemblix has a later-line/first-line
label break; Ekterly lacks a four-year actual history. Prior Kuvan/Xenazine/Orfadin
work was about generic erosion and cannot be repurposed as launch evidence.

WHAT PEAK MEANS IN THE CURRENT MODEL
Guggenheim IR2036EUR854.760m and XR2036EUR529.535m are still growing11.7% and6.9%
versus2035. They are forecast endpoints, not demonstrated saturation. The supplied
report does not disclose enough patient/price detail to reconstruct demand drivers.
Our model holds these endpoints flat until assumed generic entry, scales both with
a common valuation-solving multiplier, then applies our erosion assumptions.
At the dated31.83spot scenario and90%/90%PoS, resulting plateaus are about
USD1.002bnIR and0.621bnXR. These are required conditional sales, not independent
peak-demand forecasts. Guggenheim itself reports a-5% terminal growth assumption;
our explicit plateau/generic erosion extension is not its exact terminal method.

INDEPENDENT PEAK UNDERWRITING
XR annual revenue = sum by geography/segment of persistent paid patient-years x
net annual revenue per paid patient. IR = residual treated attacks x PHVS share x
paid doses per attack x net revenue per dose, plus paid inventory change/expiry
replacement. Prevention patients retain rescue need; improved prevention reduces
consumption, not necessarily the rescue patient count. Avoid counting free supply
as paid treatment or one-time stocking as recurring attack demand.
Project these flows annually with launch timing, switches, persistence, coverage,
net price and competition. Peak is the maximum attainable annual sales within the
commercial/exclusivity horizon; saturation need not occur before generic entry.
Use independent demand estimates to constrain reverse-solved requirements.

MODEL DECISION
Retain source curve as one transparent reference, not a calibrated central truth.
Do not replace it opportunistically. Next model extension should vary speed and
peak separately for IR/XR, hold generic dates fixed, and solve required sales across
plausible timing alternatives; test results against patient/price ceilings. No such
frontier or new independent peak estimate is claimed completed by this audit.
