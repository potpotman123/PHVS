ECONOMIC ARITHMETIC
Within12h, at most2doses: expected dose count=1+redosing fraction.
PHVS1.17 vsEkterly1.38 using rounded reported figures implies15.2%less dose consumption per treated attack. An additional17.9%treated attacks OR17.9%net revenue per dose offsets this, other factors equal. These are conditional translations of trial figures, not causal annual-sales estimates. A complete dose is20mgPHVS vs600mgEkterly; do not compare pill or milligram counts. JSON contains reproducible values.
Under fixed price per pack, fewer used doses may delay future replenishment. Under pricing aligned to attack treatment, a per-dose premium could offset; actual PHVS price/pack/discount unknown. Additional injectable rescue generates no extra oral units. Initial inventory, expiration, free supply, quantity limits and paid replenishment separate consumption from sales. All-case attack rate and fraction treated could dominate this modest per-attack effect.
