INTERPRETATION: same-disease mature prevention analogues weaken a simple XR absolute-height short. They do not establish the share left for a late entrant, willingness to switch or paid net price. Nor should their historical highs be added into a market total: product competition, overlapping patient demand, different years and geography matter.

CURRENT MODEL IMPLICATION: retain separate questions of opening scale, ramp speed, ultimate share and commercial duration. Source XR grows to614m over2028–36; source IR to992m over2027–36. Both still grow at endpoint. No automatic PHVS plateau date can be imported from the injectable histories. Patient demand model and ramp-speed/peak frontier remain required.
