NEXT MOST VALUABLE EVIDENCE

and time-at-risk; then new prescriptions -> first paid shipment -> repeat paid

THESIS IMPLICATION
Switching is real and prior oral failure does not eliminate the injectable pool
from PHVS's opportunity. But efficacy alone cannot determine adoption; preferences,
access and persistence matter. Rescue sales require actual paid replenishment,
not merely the number of people with rescue available. National adoption remains
unidentified; the evidence now makes the flow more specific and testable.
