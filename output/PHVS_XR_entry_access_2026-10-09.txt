IMPLEMENTATION / VALIDATION
xr_entry_model.py uses daily midpoint prescription cohorts across the first365days,
fixed access delay and exponential paid retention. Retention clock starts at first
paid fill; empirical61%claims proxy and74/87%assumptions remain separately visible.
One-variable sweeps isolate origin activity, conversion, lag and retention rather
than treating bundled cases as probability quantiles. Tests cover prescription
and paid stock identities, exposure bounds, spillover, zero conversion, no attrition,
and monotonicity. These verify mechanics, not external commercial validity.

LIMITATION / NEXT DECISION
This is a one-year conditional underwriting worksheet, not a selected expected
forecast, five-year adoption curve, national state-transition engine or peak-sales
estimate. No evidence establishes the low/high cases as strict bounds. Competition,
within-year reviews and changing origin stocks are represented by composite annual
rates. No systematic intra-year launch maturation or reattempt process is estimated.
Next: challenge which origin rates are supportable, then extend residual pools and
surviving cohorts over multiple years before attaching netprice and the reverseDCF.
