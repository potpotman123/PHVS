UNCERTAINTY / MONTE CARLO PREPARATION
uncertainty_register.json identifies empirical proxies, assumptions and dependencies.
Historical entries must be re-solved when retention changes. Population segments
must sum to the same total. Clinical-benefit assumptions should not independently
inflate both retention and acquisition without acknowledging their dependence.
No Monte Carlo distribution has been selected or run. Future output percentiles
will be model-implied uncertainty intervals, not empirical confidence intervals.

CHECKS AND STOPPING RULE
Verified monthly total/paid/free identities, historical fit residuals, population
accounting, zero-entry behavior and no-attrition treatment-year integration.
These validate implementation and calibration, not the commercial hypothesis.
Stopped source expansion after selected issuer/pharmacy/claims follow-ups yielded
repeated proxies rather than aligned cohorts. Preserved gaps instead of fabricating
precise current populations or conversion observations. ExistingDCF unchanged.
