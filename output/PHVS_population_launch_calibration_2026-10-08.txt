IMPLICATION
Orladeyo is evidence that a convenient oral can recruit from beyond existing
prophylaxis. PHVS must win a mix of switches and new prevention users in a changed
market; it cannot simply inherit Orladeyo's historical origin mix. Historical
net growth now provides a concrete check on proposed PHVS trajectories.

REMAINING LIMITATION / STOP
The bounded search recovered useful net-stock and origin benchmarks but not an
aligned annual paid-start series or current unique drug-level population split.
Do not fill those gaps with cumulative prescribed counts, issuer forecasts, or
survey preference. Next quantitative work should reconcile modeled gross starts,
retention and payment transitions to these historical aggregates, then explicitly
vary PHVS differentiation and competition. Access and net price still matter.
