RAPIDe-3 missing-pair audit — 6 October 2026

Decision update
The 134-to-88 gap is largely explained by preplanned closure after sufficient paired attacks accumulated. It is materially weaker as a short-thesis claim than before this audit. This supersedes earlier statements in the research memo and clinical notes that the protocol and disposition could not be located. The remaining efficacy-robustness question is the prespecified all-treated supportive analysis, whose numerical results were not found. Sponsor registry submissions are primary records, not independent verification of individual records or an FDA endorsement.

Closure explanation is now explicit
Lay summary p.5 says 35 participants left because Pharvaris had collected enough information and stopped the study. This resolves the ambiguous 'terminated or suspended by sponsor' disposition category. The results summary p.4 says the trial was not ended prematurely; p.44 says no global interruptions. These can coexist with administrative closure of participants after an information target.
Protocol pp.45–46, section6.8 expressly allows sponsor termination for final analysis after sufficient participants complete both attack periods, referencing sample-size section12.2. This is permission, not an independently audited record of exactly when management saw data or issued its closing instruction.
Protocol p.71 specifies approximately120 enrolled to obtain at least60 paired participants; states at least90% power under assumptions partly redacted. Actual88 paired exceed target60. Do not infer independently validated power or lack of bias solely from exceeding the target.
Protocol p.74 says no interim analysis applicable. No signed final SAP, unblinding log, or closing instruction obtained.

Prespecified robustness checks now identified
Protocol p.14 and pp.71–73 define PEAS as two treated attacks and SEAS as at least one treated attack. A marginal Cox proportional-hazards analysis on SEAS is prespecified, with robust variance for within-patient correlation and treatment/period/sequence terms (synopsis also includes randomization strata). Binary secondary supportive analysis uses GEE. Numerical SEAS hazard ratios, CIs and p-values were not found in the 45-page result summary or bounded targeted public searches.
Protocol also specifies supplementary treatment-policy analysis retaining assessments after rescue/prohibited medication. Primary approach treats rescue/prohibited use within12h as failure, censoring at13h upper device window regardless of prior improvement. These answer different clinical questions; rescue-use differences can contribute to headline contrast. Neither automatically invalidates the trial. Request both sets of results.
First-period-only comparison is an additional useful check; do not call it a recovered prespecified requirement. Patient-level responses, diary timestamps and missingness reason-by-sequence are not available for independent reconstruction.

Methods and limits
Bounded search of protocol identifier and trial name across company materials, US/EU registries, investigator publications and regulatory records; followed the concrete CTIS lead. Downloaded only three trial documents plus the user-provided pitch. Extracted text with page anchors, visually inspected key disposition/statistical pages, checked arithmetic and identifier against primary registry. No paid data, bulk scraping, patient identification, reconstructed Kaplan-Meier pseudo-data or fabricated sensitivity results. Further progress requires the numerical supportive analyses or more granular sponsor/regulatory disclosure.
