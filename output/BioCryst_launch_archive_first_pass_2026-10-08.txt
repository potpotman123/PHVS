INTERPRETATION / PHVS TRANSFER
1. Historical launch success included targeted physician mapping and access support,
   not merely first-oral demand. Benchmark PHVS's actual plans against these functions.
2. High-volume practices and broader treaters both mattered. Model prescriber reach,
   activation and additional patients per prescriber separately without double counting.
3. Distinguish probability of trying from retention after trying. Stable injectable
   patients may be hard to recruit but good persisters once they choose an oral.
4. Paid conversion, temporary free supply, net revenue per paid patient and retention
   require separate definitions and linked transitions. Do not multiply selected paid
   retention by an unrelated paid-stock fraction and call it a measured funnel.
5. Early versus mature discontinuation hazards should differ. Do not apply cumulative
   six/twelve-month retention repeatedly each year.
6. No national switching rate is identified: we lack the eligible incumbent denominator.
   Orladeyo first-oral appeal does not identify PHVS improved-oral capture. No model
   coefficients changed; these disclosures inform design and historical calibration.
