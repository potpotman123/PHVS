PHVS — linked US patient-adoption and revenue ramp, 9 October 2026

WHAT IS NOW BUILT
A 10-year conditional scenario model for XR and IR, using remaining origin pools,
paid-access lag, retention, modest net population growth, and net-price assumptions.
ModelYear1 is the first full IRcommercial year; XR beginsModelYear2. These are
relative years, not an updated regulatory/calendar forecast. No approval probability
applied here. Figures are US only and cannot be equated with worldwideDCFtargets.

LINKING PREVENTION AND RESCUE
Rescue cohorts overlay the same origin populations. When initiallyacute-onlyor
untreatedpatients adoptXR, their modeled rescue intensity moves towardLTPlevels.
Within eachorigin, XR/IRoverlap is proportional mixing, not observedjointbehavior.

MECHANICS AND CHECKS
Constant annual prescription probabilities apply to available residualpools.
Failed-paid prescriptions may retry after12months; successfulpaid discontinuers
remain in disease population but are not recaptured during this horizon.
No hand-fitS-curve. Depletion and retentiongenerate slowing. Growth1.5%net already
includes demographic/diagnosticbalance; noadditionaldiagnosis/death terms.
Monthly total-stock identities hold for bothproducts independently. Testedzero
conversion, launch timing, uptake/cannibalization directions andinitialsupplybudget.
These are implementation checks; the forecast is not independently validated.
