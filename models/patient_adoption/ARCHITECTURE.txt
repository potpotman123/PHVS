PHVS patient-adoption architecture — canonical design / research handoff
Updated 8 October 2026

STATUS
This is the agreed working design to investigate and calibrate, not an implemented
or validated national forecast. README.txt maps existing evidence. sensitivity.py
implements only the explicitly hypothetical closed-cohort example. Do not mistake
its assumed rates for calibrated inputs or claim the expanded model already runs.

SCOPE
US patients first; monthly transitions, annual revenue aggregation. IR = deucrictibant
on-demand; XR = deucrictibant prophylaxis. Worldwide DCF requires a separately
identified ex-US build and territorial economics. No automatic US-to-world scaling.

COMBINED FLOW
Undiagnosed HAE reservoir
  -> new diagnoses -> diagnosed treatment states below
Existing diagnosed patients --------------------------------------+
                                                                 |
  [Orladeyo | injectable prevention | other prevention |           |
   rescue-only without prevention | no current active treatment] <-+
  -> assess prevention eligibility / unmet need / preferences
  -> clinician reach and discussion
  -> consideration -> prescription -> treatment initiation
                                |            |
                                |            +-> bridge/free -> paid or discontinued
                                +-> delayed / denied / abandoned
  -> paid XR patient-months -> retained / discontinued / switched
                                      -> other states or later reattempt

Across ALL prevention states, including XR and no prevention:
  current rescue regimen + residual attack burden
  -> rescue discussion/choice -> IR prescription/access
  -> initial supply -> paid replenishment / replacement / discontinuation

Eligibility can differ between prevention and rescue. Diagnosis does not imply
immediate treatment, PHVS eligibility or PHVS capture. First-time therapy in an
already diagnosed person is not a new diagnosis. Add new entrants once only.

PATIENT STATES AND ATTRIBUTES
Maintain mutually exclusive primary prevention states at each month-end:
Orladeyo; injectable prevention (split by drug if data permit); other prevention;
no prevention; PHVS XR. Keep unknown status explicit. Subdivide no prevention
into rescue-only and no active treatment for reporting. Separately track rescue
regimen, including PHVS IR, competitor, multiple rescue products, none or unknown.
These rescue attributes overlap prevention; never sum IR and XR users as unique
patients. Short switching overlaps may be allowed explicitly, not silently counted
twice as full preventive patient-months.

Within states, clinical control, tolerability, route burden and satisfaction are
attributes, not additive populations. Use joint cells or a documented hierarchy.
Track treatment vintage, paid/free status and prior treatment for cohort analysis.
Start with a coarse segmentation; only add cells supported by consequential evidence.

XR TRANSITIONS / REVENUE
For eligible source group g at month t, a conditional funnel can be:
Rx[g,t] = N_eligible[g,t] * reach[g,t] * discussion_given_reach[g,t]
          * prescription_given_discussion[g,t].
If an observed composite prescription/adoption rate is used, omit its component
multipliers. Apply access and start probabilities with appropriate lags to these
prescription cohorts; do not apply paid-stock share as a conversion probability.
Use pending, free-start and paid-start states so a later free-to-paid conversion is
not another new patient. Record rejection, abandonment and reattempt separately.

PaidActive[t] = PaidActive[t-1] + DirectPaidStarts[t] + FreeToPaid[t]
                + PaidReturns[t] - Discontinuations[t] - PaidToFree[t] - OtherExits[t].
Terms must be mutually exclusive; entrants remove patients from their prior state.
Use cohort age-dependent discontinuation: initiation hazards versus mature hazards.
Deaths/other exits and diagnosis inflows maintain population accounting if material.

XR annual revenue = sum(monthly paid patient-equivalents * monthly net price).
Use average exposure within each month or explicit start timing, not year-end
patients multiplied by full-year price. Net pricing and free supply must have
consistent definitions; do not discount the same free product twice.

IR DISPENSING / REVENUE
IR annual revenue = sum(paid packs dispensed * net revenue per pack).
Separate initial stocking from repeat dispensing. A demand-based implementation
can use attacks * proportion treated * PHVS share of treated attacks * units per
attack, linked to inventory and pack size; consumption alone is not paid shipments.
Inventory_end = inventory_start + units supplied - units consumed - units expired.
Alternatively use observed packs/patient as a composite dispensing benchmark;
do not add consumption-based replenishment to it again. Replacement from expiration
is an explicit assumption until supported. Carrying rescue does not require frequent
attacks. Track free packs separately from paid packs and align revenue recognition.

IR/XR LINK
Prevention influences residual attacks and thus replenishment, not automatically
rescue possession. PHVS XR users can choose competing rescue; other-prevention
users can choose PHVS IR. Same-drug dual use depends on evidence/label and preferences.
Do not assume full cross-selling or one-for-one cannibalization. Joint transition
rules should keep diagnoses, unique patients and total rescue demand reconciled.

COMMERCIAL EXECUTION -> MODEL PARAMETER
Targeting / territories -> reachable practices and patient exposure.
Prescriber activation / repeat prescribing -> initiation timing and depth.
Messaging / differentiation -> consideration and prescription conditional on reach.
Hub / field access -> time to start, authorization completion and abandonment.
Contracting -> access and net price jointly.
Onboarding -> early persistence; refill support -> ongoing dispensing.
Diagnosis / referrals -> inflows, with lags and separate PHVS capture.
Choose either explicit physician-level reach or a composite observed adoption rate;
do not layer both. No arbitrary additional execution haircut.

CALIBRATION AND CHECKS
For every parameter record definition, units, population, period, numerator,
denominator, source/page, observed/derived/proxy/assumed/unknown label, range and
transferability. Source mix != switch rate; paid share != approval probability;
survey preference != adoption; selected switcher persistence != switching odds.
Aggregate first by cohort/source, then reconcile active/paid/free patient stocks,
starts/exits and revenue. Compare historical model outputs with withheld periods
where possible; arithmetic reconciliation is not independent demand validation.
Keep access, price, persistence and IR/XR overlap dependencies explicit. Use scenarios
until ranges are defensible; defer Monte Carlo distributions and fitting.
