# PHVS workbook (Crimson Capital)

`PHVS_Crimson_Capital_Model.xlsx` is the live Excel model for the Point72 Academy case. It is generated by `build_model.py` from the canonical engine inputs in `models/finalized/` and reconciled against the Python engine by `reconcile.py` and `check_cases.sh`.

## Layout

Cover (with the model map and a read-me), Summary, DCF (price target, branch probabilities, expected cash-flow walk, cost of equity), Three Statements (central case with quarters through 2031 interleaved under their annual totals, plus schedules), Reverse DCF, Operating Build (funnel from diagnosed patients to revenue with its drivers, then the supporting builds and annual drivers), Monte Carlo (distribution, branches, priors, drivers, stresses, one-variable sensitivities), INPUTS ------> (assumptions, reported financials, generic-erosion indices), Patient Engine, Financial Engine, Checks (control totals, identities, and the live four-branch engine valuation), ALT DATA ------>, Sources, and one tab per evidence dataset. The builder constructs these as separate sheets and stacks them at the end, rewriting every reference; `rowmap.json` records each logical sheet's physical tab and row offset for the reconciliation scripts.

There is no case selector: the Working column is the base case and the Override column on Inputs is the way to change an assumption.

## Build and verify

```sh
python3 models/excel/build_model.py          # writes the workbook and rowmap.json
python3 <xlsx-skill>/recalc.py models/excel/PHVS_Crimson_Capital_Model.xlsx 900   # LibreOffice recalculation
python3 models/excel/reconcile.py working    # every monthly series and branch price against working_monthly.json
./models/excel/check_cases.sh                # same for Working, Cautious and Upside in disposable copies
```

Requires Python 3.9+ with openpyxl (the agents' data tables are read from two JSON files compiled from the research archive; see `altdata` under the scratch folder recorded in the build script, or regenerate from the repo files listed on each Alt Data tab).

## Crimson underwriting case and price target

Two inputs are set below their research modes as Crimson Capital underwriting choices, recorded in `crimson_case.json` with their reasoning and flagged on the Inputs tab: PHVS launch share of oral choices 30% (research 40%; later-oral analogues 18-23%) and IR annual continuation 85% (research 90%, never measured). The central case (Three Statements, Operating Build, live DCF on Checks) runs at these values: $21.56 today, $23.76 in twelve months.

The price target is the mean 12-month value across 4,096 Monte Carlo draws on priors recentred on the Crimson values (`models/monte_carlo/expected_path.py` with `MC_CASE=models/excel/crimson_case.json`): $22.72, -26.9% against $31.10; median $22.35, P10 $14.92, P90 $30.84. The DCF tab shows the expected cash-flow path behind that mean; because discounting is linear, the DCF of the mean path reproduces the mean of the per-draw values up to the per-share step (option dilution and the zero floor), shown as a reconciling line of about 12 cents. `crimson_engine.py` regenerates the central-case monthly output, one-variable sensitivities, structural stresses and the Street-bridge waterfall; `crimson_monthly.json` is the reconciliation target for the Working case.

## Model Map and KPIs

Model Map is a layered diagram (sources, evidence registers, inputs, patient engine, economics, statements and valuation, outputs) generated by `build_model.py` into `model_map.png`. Summary carries a live KPI block of sixteen drivers and sixteen outputs; the Cover carries an eight-line headline strip. Units are stated on every row of Annual, Three Statements and Quarterly.

## Reverse DCF

The Reverse DCF tab carries the 7 October 2026 solve from `models/reverse_dcf` (required success-case ramps at the $31.83 snapshot and the $44 Guggenheim target, with the Street ramp shape, 90/90 approval odds, 12.5% discount rate and 50/50 erosion held fixed) and rescales it live to the current share price. Because equity value is linear in the common revenue scale, the two saved solves identify the slope and intercept exactly; the live row applies them to the share price on Inputs. The Street Bridge tab compares our path with Guggenheim's published path; the Reverse DCF tab compares it with what the price requires.

## Net price escalator

`price_growth` on Inputs (default 0 in every case) applies annual net price growth to both products after 2027. It exists only in the workbook; the Python engine holds net price flat, so a non-zero value flags the Monte Carlo as stale and breaks engine parity by design. At 2% a year the working point rises from $28.31 to $34.85.

## Three statements

The Three Statements tab lays out the income statement (revenue by product and geography, COGS, royalty, R&D with the milestone, fixed and variable selling, G&A, EBITDA, EBIT, tax, EPS), the condensed economic balance sheet, the cash flow statement, and supporting schedules for revenue build, operating expense build, working capital, PP&E, equity funding and share count, NOL and tax, and ratios, for 2024A-2055E. Balance, cash, PP&E and NOL checks are zero in every year. It is driven by the same monthly engine as the Annual tab; the chain is Adoption Build and Quantity & Price (the revenue projection), Revenue, Fin Monthly, Three Statements, Valuation. Monte Carlo wraps the engine inputs and reports the distribution of the valuation; it does not feed the statements.

## What recalculates and what is saved

Live formulas: Inputs (case selector, overrides), Adoption Build, XR Engine, IR Engine, Revenue, Fin Monthly (four clinical branches), Valuation, Annual, Quarterly, Quantity & Price, Checks, LOE, Calendar. Changing any input recalculates patients, revenue, cash, funding and value.

Saved engine results, labelled as such: Sensitivities (one-variable ranges, structural stresses, cases), Street Bridge value waterfall (four engine runs, one with the redosing haircut relaxed for sizing only), Monte Carlo (4,096-draw run on commit e7594c4, seed 7202609). The Monte Carlo tab flags when live inputs no longer match the simulated baseline. Refresh the simulation with `models/monte_carlo/run.py`, then rebuild.

## Reconciliation, 10 October 2026

The DCF tab carries a WACC build from public data: the 10-year Treasury par yield on 9 October 2026 (5.24%), Damodaran's January 2026 implied equity risk premium (4.23%) and his cash-corrected unlevered beta for US biotechnology (1.08), relevered at Pharvaris's zero debt, giving a CAPM cost of equity and WACC of about 9.8%. The 12% used in every Monte Carlo draw and in the central case is the Inputs assumption; the gap is shown as a premium for single-asset execution and funding risk, alongside the Street's 10% to 13% range and the engine's 10%/15% sensitivity. The annual DCF walk sums to the Monte Carlo mean.

Recalculated in LibreOffice 25.x (soffice at /opt/homebrew/bin) with 298,145 formulas and zero errors. Working, Cautious and Upside cases reproduce the engine's monthly patient-years, active members, doses, units, revenue, costs, taxes, cash, shares, equity and liquidation distributions in all four clinical branches within 1e-6, and all eight branch prices within 1e-6 per share. Control totals on the Checks tab: value today $25.8256, 12-month value $28.3116, both-approved target $31.4954, peak revenue $1,551.2m in 2038.

Method: first-year cohorts collapse exactly into a 12-month SUMPRODUCT because first-year hazard is constant; mature cohorts roll forward as one stock; access delay and retry cooldown are fixed-lag columns. XR population flows do not depend on IR; IR flows do not depend on XR except through the dose-budget overlap, so one XR ledger, one IR ledger and two IR dose sets (with and without XR) serve all four branches.

Not verified: native Microsoft Excel recalculation (no Excel on the build machine). Formulas use only SUMIFS, AVERAGEIFS, SUMPRODUCT, INDEX, MATCH, EOMONTH, MIN, MAX, LN and defined names. Excel data tables are not used, so sensitivity tables are engine-computed values rather than live two-way tables.

## Limitations carried from the engine

Comparator list prices with assumed net fractions; claims and survey benchmarks transferred as switching, preference and dose inputs; 60% mature oral share and the 1.17/1.38 redosing haircut fixed by analyst choice; funding plan is a requirement, not committed capital; economic cash-compensation statements rather than IFRS forecasts. The Inputs tab states the source and reasoning for each assumption.
