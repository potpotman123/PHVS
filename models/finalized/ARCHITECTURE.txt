PHVS MODEL ARCHITECTURE AND DATA DICTIONARY
As of 2026-10-09; valuation today 2026-10-09; target 2027-10-09.

1. Disease population and acquisition

One US diagnosed population, working 10,000 at July 2026, is partitioned into
1,700 Orladeyo, 4,080 other prevention, 2,720 acute-only and 1,500 diagnosed but
untreated. These are proxy starting pools, not a census of label-eligible users.
Age >=12 and relevant HAE subtype eligibility are absorbed into composite
capture rates: no unsupported pediatric fraction or additional AAE population
is added. The 8,500-11,500 population sensitivity changes the total and derives
all four groups from fixed weights. Do not sample the counts independently.

Net diagnosed-population growth is 1.5% annually, tested at 0-3%. It includes
new diagnoses and exits; do not add another diagnosis inflow or death outflow.
New entrants are allocated proportionately to the four original groups. This
is a compact net-growth assumption, not a demographic incidence model.

IR and XR maintain separate membership ledgers within the SAME population.
They are overlapping treatments, so their patient counts must never be added
as the number of unique people with HAE. Origin is the pre-PHVS treatment group,
not a continuously updated treatment label. Composite capture is deliberately
used instead of invented reach/preference/prescription probabilities.

For annual acquisition probability a, monthly q = 1-(1-a)^(1/12).
Prescription attempts = available people * q. Rates apply to the remaining
available pool, not the original population every year. IR working a=10% for
treated groups, 2% for diagnosed untreated. XR a=10%,4%,4%,1% respectively.

Each monthly sequence:
  (i) add net population growth to available people;
  (ii) return access failures whose cooldown expires;
  (iii) move new attempts out of available;
  (iv) after the access delay, activate conversion*attempts;
  (v) failed attempts return after 12 months from attempt;
  (vi) attrition moves active members to retired, with an optional re-entry
       stress returning 25% after 24 months.

Available + pending approval/payment + failed-access cooldown + active +
retired + re-entry cooldown = total for each product/origin/month. Retired
means no longer buying that product, not deceased or outside the disease pool.
Attempts can recur after a failure; cumulative forms are not unique patients.
No separate free-to-paid hazard is multiplied onto the conversion assumption.

2. Exposure and retention

For annual survival s, h=-ln(s), end members=start members*exp(-h/12).
Patient-years for that month=(start-end)/h; if h=0, start/12.
Cohort age is months since paid/access activation, not calendar year.

XR first 12 months: s=0.6945 =0.61+0.39*(13/30)*0.5. This retains the user's
assumed avoidance of half the efficacy-related exits. It adds 8.45 percentage
points, or 13.85% relative to 61%; it is not a measured PHVS retention rate.
No adverse-event retention benefit is assumed. Mature annual survival is 90%,
with 80-95% stress bounds; selected survivors can be more durable, but 90% is
not measured by the available initiation-cohort study.

Paid XR treatment-years = survival-integrated patient-years * paid_intensity.
The public Orladeyo study reports persistence61% and mean PDC0.73. Exponential
61% survival implies0.789 patient-years in year1. Paid_intensity=0.73/0.789,
approximately0.925, reconciles the comparator and avoids perfect refill supply.
That is a calibration transfer, not a measured conditional adherence parameter:
refill gaps/returns confound interpretation. Test0.9-1.0. Do not multiply again
by0.73. PHVS retains its efficacy-related survival improvement.

IR annual membership survival is90%, tested80-95%. Membership means maintaining
access/preference for the rescue product, not receiving monthly prescriptions.
The cited 91% repeat-dispensing statistic for Orladeyo is NOT evidence of90%
annual IR survival; it is context only. No PoS haircut occurs in cohort code.

3. Rescue demand, inventory and overlap

Doses are therapeutic administrations, not tablets, packs, attacks or Rxforms.
LTP budget8 doses/year transfers a minimal-gap prevention claims average that
includes people with no rescue claims;11.8 is the all-LTP alternative. Do not
multiply again by the67.1% rescue-claimant proportion. Non-LTP budget14 uses a
broader on-demand claimant benchmark, not a pure acute-only cohort. The missing
adopter-specific denominator is a major uncertainty, tested at10-18.

Within origin, mean XR share = XR patient-years this month*12 / population.
This mean-field share estimates overlap with IR membership. It is not observed
joint prescribing. IR active patients and XR active patients cannot be used to
infer a preference/safety rule about simultaneous use of the same molecule.

For XR share x and same-drug rescue allocation d:
  budget = (1-x)*prior-treatment dose budget + x*LTP budget*d.
  initial stock per new member = 2*((1-x)+x*d).
  replacement doses = first-year member-years*(budget-initial stock)
                    + mature member-years*budget.
  paid initial doses = new access activations*initial stock*paid-stock fraction.
  free initial doses = same stock*(1-paid-stock fraction), revenue zero.

Initial stock is inside the annual total, not added to it. If an early user
stops, previously dispensed stock is not clawed back; first-year revenue can
therefore be front-loaded relative to consumed attacks. No further redosing
haircut is applied to claims-derived dispensing. Study redose rates are not
interchangeable with commercial paid prescriptions. Pack rounding, expiration
and channel inventories are excluded until observed.

Non-PHVS prophylaxis migration is a structural stress: up to20% of original
non-LTP users gradually adopt other prevention over10years, lowering their
IR dose budget. It does not fabricate additional drug-specific switching
transitions. Constant acquisition rates and no re-entry remain transparent
base choices; dedicated valuation stresses test both simplifications.

4. Price, access and geography

XR comparator WAC = $48,487.92 per28days, annualized*365/28 (not*12).
Assumed paid-unit net fraction66% gives approximately$417,169/year.
IR comparator WAC=$16,720/dose; assumed84% net gives$14,044.80/dose.
These are independently sourced list-price anchors and explicit net discounts,
not observed PHVS prices or verified manufacturer gross-to-net percentages.
No efficacy price premium or annual list-price inflation is assumed.

Conversion determines whether an attempt ever becomes paid; net fraction
reduces revenue on paid units. Free supply never earns list-price revenue; free initial IR units retain a
manufacturing-cost allowance and shrink with brand volume after generic entry.
Do not subtract free supply from paid revenue a second time. Test paired access
packages: rebate_for_access and restricted_high_net. Univariate price/access
runs isolate arithmetic; do not sample their most favorable endpoints together
as if independent clinical evidence established both.

Commercial IR starts May2027, after the April23PDUFA; XR starts June2028,
after assumed May2028 approval following planned1H2027 applications. Both are
assumptions. First paid activation follows the separate1month access delay.
A six-month joint delay is a stress, not a promised regulatory date.

ExUS net end-market demand = mature exUS/US ratio * lagged US sales * rollout.
Ratios20%IR/25%XR,12month lag,48month rollout. Historical Orladeyo is the main
anchor; Takeda has only20%weight in the XR rationale, reflecting its stronger
global infrastructure. US and foreign net price differences are implicit in
the demand ratio; do not convert currencies again.

Base: all exUS direct sales, with overseas commercial costs. Alternative:
recognized foreign revenue = direct fraction*demand +
(1-direct fraction)*demand*partner royalty. No full partner sales plus royalty;
no unverified upfront payment/milestones. Geography ratio is a heuristic,
not a claim that comparator reported royalties equal end-market sales.

5. Calendar financials and cash

Internally monthly July2026-December2055. Quarter/year flows sum; stocks take
the last month. Exposures use standardized1/12year monthly fractions; discount
exponents use actual days/365.25. Within-month valuation/target cutoff prorates
operating cash flow; funding occurs at month-end. No elapsed burn is charged
twice. Historical2024/2025 and1H2026 remain native EUR. Opening June2026 balance
is translated once at the frozen Oct8 reference rate1.1186USD/EUR.

All operating forecasts are economic cash-compensation equivalents, not a
complete IFRS forecast. Pre-existing awards enter option/RSU dilution; their
future noncash amortization is excluded from the cash budgets. Future staff
incentives are cash-settled within budgets, so no additional SBC addback or
new-grant dilution is applied. This avoids giving SBC a free economic value.

R&D/commercial/G&A annual USDm budgets are explicit in inputs.json. For2026
only H2 is forecast;2026 annualized RD144/GA72 approximates reported1H runrates.
2027 RD130, commercial fixed100, GA45;2028 RD105/commercial120/GA50. R&D then
falls85/70/60 by2031 while ongoing medical/development work is retained.
Commercial also costs8%US direct sales+18%foreign direct sales. Fixed costs
inflate2% after2031 untilLOE. KalVista launch SG&A and BioCryst mature expenses
are reasonableness anchors, not a fitted PHVS margin or copied broker model.

Manufacturing/distribution costs4% of direct sales; separate BRAIN royalty3%
of worldwide direct/indirect end-market sales. Low-to-mid-single-digit range
comes from the filing; exact tiers unknown. No post-generic royalty relief
credited. Remaining EUR7m milestone reserve (EUR8m atFY2025 lessEUR1m inH1)
is conservatively paid at assumed IR approval; exact due dates are unknown.

Capex=$0.5m/year plus0.5%direct sales; assets depreciate over a rolling5-year
proxy. NWC=opening operating NWC+12% annualized monthly direct sales. Opening
negative NWC is retained, not magically released at launch. One-month sales
annualization is a compact receivables/inventory/payables proxy.

Taxes:20% normalized positive taxable profit. No historical unrecognized NOL
asset credited. Forecast losses accumulate a pooled NOL with80% utilization
cap, plus$1m/year operating tax floor. This is a planning proxy, not a legal
jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction tax opinion. Sensitivity15-25.8%.

FCF=net income+D&A-capex-change inNWC (no forecast interest/FX). Cash rolls
from opening cash+FCF+net financing; net assets= cash+PPE+NWC+other net assets.
Net assets reconcile to equity plus net income and capital contributions.
Term debt0. Small leases remain in opening other net assets and operating
budgets, not deducted twice from equity.

Financing: when modeled cash falls below$75m, raise to$150m. Gross proceeds=
needednet/(1-5%fee). Illustrative issuance$25/share determines statement share
counts and realized pre-target dilution. This is a funding requirement, not
committed or assured capital. Cautious losses can imply uneconomic continued
funding; zero equity value and required capital must both be shown.

DCF AFTER the valuation/target date assumes future funding at fair value;
future operating losses are already deducted from EV. It charges financing
fees, but does not divide again by ALL future diagnostic shares: that would
charge the same cash need twice. The diagnostic fixed-price financing table
beyond the target is not the DCF capital-pricing assumption. If modeling
future discounted placements, implement an explicit ownership/issuance event
and replace fair funding; do not simply add a dilution haircut to negativeFCF.

6. Clinical risk, equity value and post-patent economics

Clinical states: both, IRonly, XRonly, neither. MarginalPoS90%each; sharedgate95%
is a dependence scenario, not empirical correlation. P(both)=.9*.9/.95;
remaining weights follow marginals. Independent and perfectly correlated
alternatives are reported. Failed products earn no sales. Product-linked R&D
and shared platform costs are reduced following decision dates; necessary
costs remain. Neither-success branch winds down6months after XRdecision and
returns residual cash after liabilities; it does not retain an infinite shell.

Risk-adjusted equity/share = sum(stateprobability * state equity/share).
Cash is not multiplied byPoS. Costs are branch-specific, not universally
multiplied by90%. Do not haircut revenue AND final equity again. The target
is an expected distribution of future clinical outcomes using today's beliefs,
not a claim that approval probability will remain90% after an FDA decision.

DCF uses nominalUSD12% discounting, no terminal exit multiple. Equity=operating
PV+cash-fees; zero floor. Existing RSUs1.545152m are included conservatively.
Existing options4.455397m at EUR12.56 use treasury-stock algebra at model price:
if in the money, price=(equity+options*strike)/(ordinary+RSUs+options).
Loss-period accounting diluted EPS does not eliminate economic dilution.
