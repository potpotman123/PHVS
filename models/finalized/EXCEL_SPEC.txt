EXCEL IMPLEMENTATION CONTRACT

Build a new workbook from these JSON inputs and equations. This is a calculation
contract, not a prescribed layout. Do not reuse a broker workbook.

Suggested tabs, in dependency order:

01 Readme / 02 Sources / 03 Inputs
  Display valuation and target dates, currencies, units and scenario selector.
  Import source IDs, URLs, dates, locators, observations and limitations. Each
  input needs its value, units, denominator, bounds, status and rationale.
  Keep scenario overrides separate from observed facts. Link every assumption
  to a canonical input cell; do not hardcode the same assumption twice.

04 History
  Import 2024/2025 annual actuals and H1 2026 from history.json in EUR millions.
  Preserve the reported cash-flow, balance-sheet, SBC and FX fields. Forecasts
  start from June 30, 2026 balances. If historical dollar financials are wanted,
  obtain historical average and balance-date FX separately; do not use the
  frozen forecast FX rate to calculate historical dollar growth.

05 Calendar
  One column per month, July 2026–December 2055. Identify year, quarter, launch
  flags, clinical decisions, commercial age, generic-entry age and days from
  both valuation dates. Use actual days/365.25 for discounting and 1/12 year
  for monthly cohort exposure. Operating flows are prorated at a partial-month
  valuation cutoff; financing occurs at month-end.

06 XR Cohorts / 07 IR Cohorts
  Separate matrices for nonzero prevention-origin x primary-rescue-origin cells;
  one row per activation cohort. Import segmented_adoption.py formulas and
  inputs.json segmented_constants. Rates depend on each product launch age.
  Do not use legacy ir_rx/xr_* acquisition parameters in segmented mode.
  Track available people, growth, attempts, pending access, failures, cooldown
  returns, active members, exits and retired members. Follow Population.step
  in engine.py. Monthly acquisition = 1-(1-annual probability)^(1/12).
  Survivor = opening members * annual retention^(1/12).
  Exposure = (opening-survivor)/(-LN(retention)); at retention=1 use opening/12.
  Change XR retention after cohort age 12 months, not each January.
  IR membership is access/preference, not a monthly dispensing prescription.

08 Rescue / Overlap
  Within each origin, XR share = XR patient-years * 12 / population.
  Implement the budget, initial stock and replacement formulas in ARCHITECTURE.
  Deduct all initial stock from the annual first-year supply budget, including
  stock supplied free. Free stock generates no revenue. Do not apply additional
  rescue-claimant discounts. Redosing quantity_multiplier=1.17/1.38 in every case;
  fix this input and preserve initial stock. Net-price multipliers
  are fixed1 and must not alter paid units or adoption mechanically.
  Do not sum overlapping IR/XR users to infer unique disease patients.

09 Revenue / Geography
  XR paid exposure = survival exposure * paid-intensity adjustment.
  IR paid doses = paid initial stock + replacement doses.
  XR annual price = 28-day WAC * 365/28 * net fraction; IR = WAC/dose * net fraction.
  Revenue in USDm = quantity * net price / 1,000,000.
  Overseas demand uses lagged US sales and a rollout phase. Separate direct
  sales from partner royalties; never recognize both on the same sales.

10 Post-patent Economics
  Use separate IR/XR generic-entry dates and protected-year quantity anchors.
  Blend revenue indices and quantity indices 50/50; effective price = R/Q.
  Do not multiply simple averages of price and quantity indices. Freeze the
  foreign anchor separately. After exposure year 4, hold price flat and let
  quantity decline. Operating cost runoff follows with a one-year lag.
  Raw cohort patient fields become counterfactual after generic entry. Use
  IR_units / XR_units, including erosion, to calculate monetized revenue.

11 Costs / 12 Financial Statements
  Import the explicit annual R&D, commercial and G&A budgets. Add variable
  commercial costs, manufacturing and BRAIN royalties separately. Calculate
  milestone reserves, capex, depreciation, working capital and taxes.
  Forecasts use cash-compensation equivalents; do not add back assumed SBC
  while also omitting future employee compensation or existing award dilution.
  Show 2026 actual/forecast bridge, full 2027–2031 forecasts and at least 2027
  and 2028 quarterly. Keep the annual extension through 2055 for valuation.
  Flows sum; stocks take the last period. Calculate patient-years directly
  from cohorts rather than averaging year-end patient counts.
  CFO = net income + D&A - change in NWC; FCF = CFO - capex.
  Cash + PPE + NWC + other net assets must reconcile to equity.

13 Funding / Shares
  Show opening cash, ordinary shares, options and RSUs; required new capital;
  gross/net proceeds, fees and issued shares. Shares issued = gross proceeds /
  assumed issue price. Funding is a requirement, not a financing commitment.
  Before the target, scenario financing enters cash and shares. Beyond the
  target, the DCF assumes fair-value funding and already deducts operating
  cash losses; do not charge all diagnostic future dilution again.
  A future discounted-placement assumption requires an explicit ownership
  event module replacing fair issuance, not an extra blanket dilution haircut.

14 Clinical Outcomes
  Separate both-success, IR-only, XR-only and neither-success cases. Branch
  costs as well as revenue; retain necessary development and commercial costs.
  Use clinical_weights to preserve marginal PoS. No revenue from a failed
  product and no PoS haircut to cash. Neither-success winds down and returns
  residual cash; no indefinitely burning shell.

15 Valuation
  Calculate directly at October 9, 2026 and October 9, 2027. Include partial
  months, explicit patent erosion and the declining residual after 2055.
  Terminal value = FCF_2055*(1+g)/(discount rate-g), discounted from end-2055.
  Apply option treasury-stock algebra at model value, not loss-period EPS.
  Weight clinical-state equity/share outcomes. Do not apply another 90%
  haircut to both revenue and equity. Show conditional and expected values.

16 Sensitivities / 17 Standalone Monte Carlo
  Match deterministic outputs first. Follow monte_carlo_spec.json for sampling
  dependencies. Acquisition has been removed from the scope.
  Ranges and simulation quantiles are underwriting, not statistical confidence
  intervals. Report standalone capital requirements.

Acceptance criteria:
  - Reproduce annual revenues within $0.01m and prices within $0.01/share.
  - Reconcile monthly, quarterly and annual flows; preserve stock balances.
  - Match zero-conversion, delayed-launch, survival, supply-budget, geographic,
    clinical-weight and patent tests in validate.py.
  - Retain precision in calculations and round only display cells.
  - Resolve every source ID. Record deviations in a reconciliation table;
    never change the Python benchmark silently to match an Excel error.
  - Passing checks demonstrates arithmetic consistency, not market accuracy.
