Cross these with preventive background; do not add them to XR people.

EVIDENCE TRANSLATION
Claims50/328switchers establish a mixed-initiator any-LTP-switch benchmark, not
PHVS-specific demand. Higher pre-index rescue dispensing among later switchers
supports burden-sensitive acquisition, without identifying its magnitude.
OASISplus includes controlled patients who chose to switch; dissatisfaction cannot
be the only entry route. The11formerOrladeyo participants are selected switchers,
not an estimate of dissatisfied users in the national installed base.
Expert calls distinguish control, administration preference and office/access
friction. Their percentages have no shared horizon/denominator and are not averaged.
Berolife13/80efficacy cessations among starters inform retention mechanisms, not
16.25%poorcontrol prevalence among continuingOrladeyo users.

FORMULAS AND ACCESS
Per source group: available people x monthly prescription probability -> attempts;
then paid conversion and delay -> paid starters -> retention/quantity -> revenue.
Use one mutually exclusive control-status partition; avoid stacking preference,
dissatisfaction and historical switching percentages from unrelated populations.
Maintain80%paidconversion and1monthdelay as shared provisional assumptions. Route
preference is not a coverage adjustment. Historical filled-switch rates include access.
Net population growth remains1.5%; newly diagnosed patients are not added twice.
