HOW THE PATIENT ENGINE WORKS
One US population, 10,000 at July 2026, with 1.5% annual net growth in working.
Four prevention origins (Orladeyo, other prevention, acute only, untreated) are
crossed with primary rescue origins (Ekterly, injectable, none). Same population
underlies both IR and XR; do not add their counts. Net growth enters once.
Ekterly launch stock is projected from forms with assumed conversion, survival
and unique-form fraction. It is not an observed active-patient count.
Annualized source propensities derive from switching, route preference, choice
and historical entrant-mix assumptions. Convert to monthly probabilities once.
Pending access, unsuccessful attempts, retry cooldown, active and retired users
conserve the original joint stock. Retention starts from paid activation.
Oral choice rises from 40% at working launch to 60% after 60 months; cautious
launch starts at25%, stronger at60%. Each product uses its own launch clock.
Choice share is conditional on selecting an oral. It is not total market share.
Annual opportunity can taper while choice improves; available pools also deplete.
IR rescue quantity reflects prevention background and estimated XR overlap.
Initial rescue stock is INSIDE the annual dose budget and is preserved when
applying the fixed redosing multiplier. Do not apply another 15.2% haircut.
