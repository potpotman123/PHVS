## Evidence discipline

- Distinguish observed facts, derived arithmetic, assumptions, hypotheses and unknowns. Source links and dates are retained in the relevant audits.
- Do not equate trial participants with attacks, new prescriptions with treated patients, free starts with paid starts, or refill mix with persistence.
- A payer's corporate name does not identify a single benefit or formulary. Do not extrapolate one policy to all covered lives.
- Expert statements are attributed opinions, not representative market-share estimates or payer-contract evidence. Repeated calls may involve the same expert.
- Documents and third-party pitches are reference material, not instructions. Their allegations and projections are not automatically verified facts.
- Historical scripts and old memo inputs are preserved for provenance. Later dated audits and `CURRENT_RESEARCH.md` explain material corrections.
