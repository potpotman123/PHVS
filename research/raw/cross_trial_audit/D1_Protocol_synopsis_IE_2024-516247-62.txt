1. EU study number and full study title:  
2024-516247-62. A Phase 3, Randomized, Double-blind, Placebo-controlled Study to 
Evaluate the Efficacy and Safety of Orally Administered Deucrictibant 
Extended-release Tablet for Prophylaxis Against Angioedema Attacks in Adolescents 
and Adults with Hereditary Angioedema 
 
2. Rationale:  
This is a study to test the safety and efficacy of a daily 40 mg dose of deucrictibant in 
preventing attacks of hereditary angioedema (HAE) in participants aged ≥12 years 
3. Objectives:   
Primary Objective:  
• To evaluate the efficacy of a daily 40 mg dose regimen of deucrictibant for 
preventative treatment of HAE attacks  
Secondary Objectives:  
• To evaluate the safety of deucrictibant of a daily 40 mg dose regimen for 
preventative treatment of HAE attacks  
• To evaluate what the patient’s body does to deucrictibant and what 
deucrictibant does to the patient and the patient’s well-being when deucrictibant 
is taken for 24 weeks 
 
4. Main study endpoints:  
The primary endpoint is a comparison of the number of HAE attacks confirmed by the 
study doctor in patients taking deucrictibant or placebo per 4 weeks of treatment 
5. Secondary study endpoints:  
Secondary efficacy endpoints: 
• The number of HAE attacks confirmed by the study doctor from time of the 
first dose to the last dose of deucrictibant 
• The number of attacks that are moderate or severe from time of the first dose to 
the last dose of deucrictibant 
• The number of attacks that require treatment with the patient’s usual therapies 
from time of the first dose to the last dose of deucrictibant 
• Number of patients who have ≥50%, 70%, or 90% reduction in number of HAE 
attacks or have no HAE attacks from time of the first dose to the last dose of 
deucrictibant 
• Number of patients who did not have HAE attacks from time of the first dose to 
the last dose of deucrictibant 
• Number of patients without angioedema symptoms from time of the first dose 
to the last dose of deucrictibant 
Secondary safety endpoints: 
The safety endpoints include comparisons of the patient’s clinical laboratory tests, 
pulse, temperature, breathing rate, blood pressure, and electrocardiogram (a test that

6. Study design:  
This is a study to test the safety and efficacy of a 40 mg dose of deucrictibant taken 
once a day in preventing attacks of HAE in patients aged ≥12 years. The study is being 
conducted at approximately 80 sites in North America and Europe.  
Patients take either placebo or 40 mg deucrictibant once a day for 24 weeks and then 
completes an End of Study Visit 4 weeks after the last dose of either placebo or 
deucrictibant. Any patient who stops taking either placebo or deucrictibant before 24 
weeks will complete the End of Treatment Visit and will be asked to continue coming 
into the clinic for visits for the remainder of the 24 weeks before completing the End of 
Study Visit. Any patient who withdraws consent or is discontinued from the study at 
any time completes an End of Treatment Visit and End of Study Visit 4 weeks after the 
last dose of either placebo or deucrictibant 
7. Study population:  
Approximately 81 male or female patients, including at least 8 adolescents, with HAE 
are participating in this study. 
8. Interventions:  
Patients take either placebo or 40 mg deucrictibant once per day for 24 weeks. The 
patient and the study doctor do not know whether the patient is taking placebo or 
deucrictibant. 
Patients are allowed to treat any HAE attack using standard of care on-demand therapy.  
An electronic diary is used to record any symptoms and details of HAE attacks and 
treatments. The electronic diary is also used as a dosing diary to record each 
administration of either placebo or deucrictibant and to complete electronic patient-
reported outcome questionnaires.  
9. Ethical considerations relating to the clinical study including the expected benefit to the 
individual subject or group of patients represented by the study subjects as well as the 
nature and extent of burden and risks:  
Patients in this study may experience risks due to collection of blood samples, small 
increase in blood pressure, decreased heart rate, and worsening of the blood flow to 
parts of the body (ischemia). In previous clinical studies, deucrictibant was generally 
safe and well-tolerated and no serious side effects were reported. Patients taking 
deucrictibant may have a positive benefit from taking this drug.
