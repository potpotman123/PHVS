      Our view: Pharvaris reported an 83% attack-rate reduction versus placebo in 
      CHAPTER-3. Focusing on the 80 patients with HAE types 1 and 2, efficacy improved
      further to an 87% attack-rate reduction, broadly in line with the current injectable
      standard of care, Takhzyro (see table below). Ahead of the readout, our base case
      assumed an 80-85% reduction, and the reported data came in comfortably within
      that range. We believe these results clearly differentiate deucrictibant from Orladeyo
      on efficacy and support its potential to become the preferred first-line prophylactic
      treatment for newly diagnosed HAE patients. We expect a positive share price
      reaction today. Consequently, we raise our probability of success for deucrictibant in
      prophylactic HAE to 90%, increase our target price to $ 49, and reiterate our Buy
      rating.

       Business Description for: Pharvaris                                        
       Pharvaris is a Dutch biotech developing deucrictibant, an oral therapy for both acute and
       long-term prophylactic treatment of hereditary angioedema (HAE). A phase 3 study
       (RAPIDe-3) in the acute setting has completed enrolment with a readout expected in 1Q26.
       In addition, Pharvaris has initiated a phase 3 (CHAPTER-3) study in the prophylactic setting
       with a targeted enrolment of 81 patients and a readout is expected in 2H26.

      The price target for Pharvaris is based on the following parameters:        
      Pharvaris is a Dutch biotech developing deucrictibant for HAE, a rare genetic disease with a
      $3b market, in both the on-demand and prophylactic settings. We see Pharvaris as an
      attractive investment case underpinned by deucrictibant’s de-risked B2R target, competitive
      phase 2 results setting it up to be the only high efficacy oral option, in addition to a
      validated commercial opportunity for more convenient orals as evidenced by competitor
      Orladeyos’s successful launch despite sub-par efficacy. We are confident in the upcoming
      phase 3 readouts in HAE on-demand (1Q26) and prophylaxis (2H26).
