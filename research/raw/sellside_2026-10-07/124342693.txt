                                       Quantifying Orladeyo Erosion - What the      
       1 Year Price History                                                         
                                       Market is Implying at $8.4/sh                
                                       The #1 FAQ we get from investors regarding HAE/PHVS
                                                                                    
                                       competition is “what is priced in here?”. We hear a lot of
                                       narratives (on both sides), but not a lot of #s.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                       Clearly at ~$8/sh, the stock is not reflecting what’s in consensus
       Source: FactSet                                                              
                                       models (avg analyst PT ~$20/sh). And given LOE
                                       timelines/different product growth profiles, it’s hard to use
                                       peak sales multiples. You really need an accurate DCF.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                       So that’s our goal with this note. We built a reverse
                                       engineered DCF in order to back into what is being implied by
                                       the market at the current valuation and frame risk/reward.
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                       At $8.4/sh, mkt cap = $2.5B, and EV = $2.4B  
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                       Here is how we back into $8.4/sh in a DCF:   
                                                                                    
                                          ➢ Orladeyo: peak WW sales of $685M in '27, declining to
                                            ~$550M by '37, before LOE in '38. Pts on paid drug
                                            peaks at ~1,200 in '27 and rolls down to ~700 (almost
                                            half of peak) by '37, reflecting prophy share erosion
                                            from 25% to low-teens. V heavy erosion  
                                                                                    
                                          ➢ Navenibart: WW unadj/risk-adj peak sales of
                                            $530M/$460M, which plateaus by early/mid 2030s,
                                            rather than continuing to grow. ~half of consensus
                                                                                    
                                          ➢ Operating margins: ramps from ~high-20%’s now to
                                            50%-55% at peak that is held steady into ’37 before
                                            Orla LOE, after which margins back in the high teens

                                          ➢ Valuation: 10.5% discount rate, DCF through 2045 with
                                            no terminal growth/value beyond that    
                                          ➢ Pipeline: ascribe 0 credit to Netherton, no additional
                                                                                    
                                            “pipeline value” plug                   
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                       To summarize, we think this stock price is reflecting ~$1B peak
                                       sales at ~60% peak operating margin = $2.4B EV and equates
                                       to a 2.2x peak sales multiple.               
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                       So, what gets you upside from the current valuation, we see a
                                       few scenarios:                               
                                                                                    
                                          ➢ Orladeyo's patient base is stickier than implied. $8
                                            implies the pt base falls ~40% off peak by '37. You don't
                                            need growth, even modeling patients down every year,
                                                                                    
                                            erosion of 20% off peak instead of 40% reflects an
                                            additional $2/sh of value               
                                          ➢ Navenibart P3 in 3Q27 is a high PoS event and unlocks
                                                                                    
                                            the next leg of growth for the company. Adding even
                                            $100M (risk adj from 85% to 100%) reflects an
                                            additional $2/sh of value. We have ~$1B in our base
                                            case, driving the upside in our model   
                                                                                    
                                          ➢ Netherton is free, with the readout expected by YE26
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                       Can send this reverse DCF + our model ($17 PT) if you want to
                                       play around with them.
