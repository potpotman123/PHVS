     Investment Thesis, Valuation and Risks                                         
     Pharvaris N.V. (PHVS)                                                          
     Investment Thesis                                                              
     Our Overweight rating on PHVS reflects our view that deucrictibant’s strong on-demand efficacy can drive meaningful uptake, displacing
     injectable therapies and EKTERLY. We also expect Phase 3 prophylaxis data to replicate Phase 2 results, supporting broad market capture
     from existing oral prophylaxis therapies and high use in new starts.           
     Target Price Valuation for PHVS                                                
     Our $65 price target is based on a probability-adjusted DCF sum-of-the-parts analysis of deucrictibant IR and XR for HAE and AAE-
     C1INH, assuming a 10% discount rate and no terminal growth. A 90% PoS was chosen to account for uncertainty in liver signal, awaiting
     more data from the OLE.                                                        
     Risks to Our Price Target and Rating for PHVS                                  
     Key risks include safety signals in the CHAPTER-4 OLE and/or the Phase 3 CREAATE study in the prophylaxis setting, failure to secure FDA
     approvals in either the on-demand or the prophylaxis setting, potential stickiness of patients to current HAE therapies, stronger-than-
     expected shift of patient preference toward injectable prophylaxis options with long dosing intervals (Q3M or Q6M) or one-and-done gene
     editing that are currently being developed by competitors.
