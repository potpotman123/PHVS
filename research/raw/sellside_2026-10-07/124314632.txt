                         (Continued from cover)... Our recent doc feedback suggests that switching to XR from Orladeyo
                         could occur rapidly – we now model that Orladeyo market share could erode ~30% from 2027
                         peak within 4 years, with sales of ~$450M in 2031 (vs. cons. $892M), after which we see sales
                         stabilizing into the out-years.                            
                                                                                    
                         Feedback remains positive on the long-acting injectable opportunity for navenibart, and
                         validated target and construct help PoS, but data still likely some ways away. We have been
                         positive on BCRX's Astria acquisition for navenibart, which targets a validated kallikrein pathway
                         and where prior data has been strong – despite small n – and suggestive of potential Q3-6 month
                         administration. Our doc checks have similarly indicated high levels of excitement in the long-
                         acting injectable agent, with our recent survey finding that docs would consider switching ~33%
                         of their HAE patients currently well-managed on other injectables to navenibart, which we est.
                         will achieve $542M in out-year sales. That said, it will be some time before de-risking ph.III data
                         (3Q27), and we will likely see Orladeyo commercial erosion before navenibart's potential launch;
                         we also believe that the greater Orladeyo commercial risks now place more pressure on strong
                         navenibart results that compete well with other emerging LAIs for BCRX to optimally maintain
                         their HAE franchise.
