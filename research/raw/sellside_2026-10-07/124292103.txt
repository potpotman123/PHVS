     ● Details on the safety profile from the call:                                 
        ○ There were 3 Gr3 AEs: 1 tooth infection and 2 "Level 2" liver             
         enzyme elevations. Per mgmt, these elevations were 'slightly               
         above' 5xULN and asymptomatic with no elevated billirubin. Both            
         patients had confounding factors: either long term androgen                
         use or fatty liver disease. One patient discontinued treatment             
         and the other continued treatment and completed the study. The             
         discontinuation in the pbo arm was due to urticaria.                       
        ○ There was 1 treatment emergent Gr4 SAE: laryngeal dyskinesia              
         in a patient with piror history of repeated laryngeal intubation           
         which was determined to be unrelated to treatment.                         
        ○ There were no GI side effects, and GI emergent AEs were                   
         balanced between treatment and pbo. Most common AEs were                   
         upper respiratory tract infections, nasopharyngitis and headache.

        ● Key points from the press release                                         
                                                                                    
           ○ Treatment with deucrictibant XR in participants with all types of      
             hereditary angioedema (HAE) reduced the mean monthly attack            
             rate by 83% (p<0.0001).                                                
           ○ Further analysis in the 80 participants with HAE Type 1 or Type 2      
             showed that the mean monthly attack rate was 87% lower in the          
             deucrictibant XR group v. pbo.                                         
           ○ All secondary efficacy endpoints, assessed sequentially under a        
             multiplicity-control procedure, were also stat sig.                    
                                                                                    
           ○ In CHAPTER-3, administration of deucrictibant XR resulted              
             in early-onset protection within the first week, which was then        
             sustained through the 24 weeks of study treatment.                     
           ○ Safety: In CHAPTER-3, deucrictibant XR was well tolerated              
             with most treatment-emergent adverse events being mild or              
             moderate. There were no treatment-related serious adverse              
             events reported. One participant in each group discontinued            
             treatment due to an adverse event.                                     
           ○ Next Steps: CHAPTER-4 OLE study of deucrictibant XR for the            
             prophylaxis of HAE attacks is ongoing. Topline data from Part 1        
             of CREAATE (NCT07266805), a global, pivotal Phase 3 study              
             evaluating orally-administered deucrictibant XR for the prevention     
             of AAE-C1INH attacks, are anticipated in the first quarter of 2027.    
             Enrollment in CREAATE is ongoing and progressing as planned.           
             PHVS plans to submit an NDA for prophylaxis deucrictibant in           
             1H27.

        Valuation                                                                   
                                                                                    
        We derive a one-year PT of $50 for PHVS based on a DCF analysis using a 13% discount rate and a 2% terminal
        growth rate, converting the implied market cap from EUR to USD by using a 6-month FX average, representing an
        implied ~$3.4B market capitalization. Our valuation is driven by probability-adjusted revenue for the two different
        formulations of deucrictibant for HAE. We ascribe a 95% PoS for on-demand and a 60% PoS for prophylaxis.
                                                                                    
        Risks to Valuation                                                          
                                                                                    
        As a late-clinical-stage company, risks to PHVS include potential clinical, regulatory, and financial setbacks.
        Promising early clinical data may not translate to success in later-stage development. Regulatory decisions are
        unpredictable and may adversely affect the commercial potential of Pharvaris product candidates. Competition in
        the hereditary angioedema space (e.g., Takhzyro, Haegarda, Ekterly, Orladeyo) may limit commercial success(es)
        of Pharvaris products, if approved. From a financial perspective, dilutive financing is a potential risk. Other
        unanticipated situations could arise that may negatively or positively impact the stock.
