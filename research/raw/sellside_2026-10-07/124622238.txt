                             Investment   Thesis,  Valuation and  Risks             
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                             BioCryst Pharmaceuticals (Neutral; Price Target: $13.00)
                             Investment Thesis                                      
                             While we see some upside to valuation based on the existing business alone, we think the
                             path to upside for BCRX in the near/medium term is heavily dependent on the navenibart
                             launch and the market’s view of its future deal(s), and we would prefer to get closer to the
                             navenibart launch and/or see what BCRX’s next deal looks like before becoming more
                             constructive on the stock. Maintain Neutral rating.    
                             Valuation                                              
                             Raising December 2027 PT to $13 from $12 on higher Orladeyo revenue. Our price
                             target is DCF-derived and reflects credit from Orladeyo and risk-adjusted pipeline credit.
                             We use a WACC of 10.0% and 0% terminal growth rate.    
                                                                                    
                             Risks to Rating and Price Target                       
                             BCRX is susceptible to the standard risks that apply to the entire biotech industry, including
                             development, regulatory, commercial, manufacturing, financing, and IP pitfalls. Risks
                             more specific to BCRX include failure to successfully commercialize and grow navenibart,
                             emergence of competition that takes market share from Orladeyo, and pipeline setbacks.
                             Upside risks include value-enhancing biz dev, better-than-expected Orladeyo performance,
                             and better ph III data/faster-than-expected ramp for navenibart.
