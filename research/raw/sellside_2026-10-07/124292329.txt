    Model changes                                                                   
    In our DCF, we roll the quarter and increase our prophy program LoS to 70% (prev. 50%)
    in-line with how we generally model positive phase 3 studies. We also push out the
    launch to 2028 (prev. late-27) in-line with the planned 1H27 filing timeline. We also
    increase our operating expenses in-line with our new launch timelines and ongoing
    CHAPTER-4 open label extension study. The prophy program now contributes $21/sh 
    (prev. $15/sh) to our new $44 PO (prev. $38). We assign $35/sh to HAE programs in both
    prophylaxis and acute on-demand settings (previous $29/sh).

    Price objective basis & risk                                                    
                                                                                    
    Pharvaris (PHVS)                                                                
    Our $44 PO is based on our DCF-based valuation with $35/sh assigned to (hereditary
    angioedema) HAE programs in both prophylaxis and acute on-demand settings. The  
    remainder of our valuation comes from pipeline and corporate expenses, and cash which
    offset. Our assumptions are based on 10% WACC for the acute on-demand program and
    prophylaxis program, 6% COGS, 15-20% GTN, and 10%-20% peak penetrations in target
    indications.                                                                    
    Upside risks to our price objective are: 1) positive data and additional validation in HAE,
    2) superior efficacy or safety profile compared to standard of care or other competitors
    in the pipeline, 3) pipeline expansion to other indications outside of HAE.     
                                                                                    
    Downside risks are: 1) failure to show efficacy in HAE, 2) unexpected side effect, 3)
    inferior efficacy or safety profile compared to standard of care or other competitors, and
    4) competitors entry in HAE.
