                    Pharvaris (PHVS)                                                
                    On To The Next CHAPTER-3; Raising Target To $50                 
                                                                                    
                    The Wedbush View: Remain Outperform rated on PHVS, new $50 target. The CHAPTER-3
                    readout landed in-line with our expectations as deucrictibant XR demonstrated an 83% reduction
                    in monthly attack rates versus placebo. On secondary endpoints like attack-free interval,
                    deucrictibant XR looks similar vs. Takhzyro with 45.5% of patients attack free from weeks 1-24 vs.
                    44% for Takhzyro. Overall safety appears uneventful and while there were two cases of liver
                    enzyme elevations, we spoke to PHVS who indicated one of these subjects had concomitant fatty
                                                                                    
                    liver disease. Bottom line, we see the data appearing robust overall for PHVS and we are
                    increasing our deucrictibant XR gross pricing assumption to $45k/month. Staying consistent with
                    our valuation methodology, our PHVS target increases to $50 (prior $42). We also see read
                    through to Outperform-rated BCRX and with our base case already assuming deucrictibant XR
                    reaches the market, we anticipate patients already controlled on Orladeyo will be less likely to
                    switch. No change to our Outperform rating on BCRX and PHVS.    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                                                                                    
                    Key Takeaways – CHAPTER-3 Highlights                            
                    PHVS reported top-line results from the CHAPTER-3 study of deucrictibant XR. Most important,
                    the primary endpoint was met with deucrictibant XR lowering monthly attack rates by 83% vs.
                    placebo (see figure below).

                    Model Updates – Increasing Deucrictibant XR Pricing Assumptions 
                    Following the CHAPTER-3 update, we are increasing our pricing assumption for deucrictibant XR.
                    Recently priced prophy drugs have been >$50k/month and with deucrictibant XR demonstrating
                    Takhzyro-like efficacy, we are increasing our estimate to $45k/month on a gross basis. The figure
                    shown below illustrates our revised pricing and we estimate net annual pricing of ~$418k for
                    deucrictibant XR.

                    commercial launch. With the data now in hand, nothing changes in our assumptions for BCRX.

     Valuation                                                                      
     Our PHVS target derives from a sum-of-parts DCF analysis which examines potential PHVS416 and PHVS719 cash flows, discounted
     back by 15% per year.                                                          
     Company Description                                                            
     Pharvaris N.V. (PHVS) is headquartered in Switzerland, with additional presence in the Netherlands and Boston. Deucrictibant represents
     the most advanced asset and is being developed for the treatment of hereditary angioedema attacks.
                                                                                    
     Risks to the Attainment of Our Price Target and Rating:                        
     ● Clinical. Data emerging from clinical studies can significantly impact shares in either a positive or negative direction.
     ● Regulatory. PHVS may employ accelerated strategies to gain approval. Regulators may require additional data or could alter their
       requirements for approval, necessitating additional or longer studies. Failure to gain product labels that lack certain features could
       limit PHVS' ability to gain traction in the market.                          
     ● Commercial. Failure to execute commercially or inability to meet/exceed expectations can impact shares.
     ● Competitive. Updates from competitors may positively or negatively impact shares.
     ● Financial. PHVS will need to access the capital markets to complete development and commercialization of their assets. If they are
       unable to raise adequate funds, this could impact their ability to continue advancing their pipeline.
