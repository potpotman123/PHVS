    Overall, these data are broadly confirmatory of deucrictibant's efficacy profile established
    in prior studies, and thus largely in-line with expectations. The results reinforce that
    deucrictibant could ultimately become a competitive oral prophylactic option, particularly
    for patients who value oral administration but remain incompletely controlled on Orladeyo.
    On the conf call, the HAE KOL similarly noted that patients doing very well on Orladeyo may
    have little reason to switch, while patients seeking greater efficacy could represent potential
    deucrictibant candidates.                                                       
    We believe BCRX's navenibart remains well positioned as a complementary opportunity for
    BCRX through its potential Q3M/Q6M dosing profile, which could offer a distinct value
    proposition for patients prioritizing high efficacy with very infrequent administration.
    Bottom Line: We view deucricitbant's CHAPTER-3 results as largely in line with expectations
    and not thesis-changing for BCRX. Mgmt had long expected PHVS to report ph 3 data that
    would be competitive with Orladeyo, and most investors had expected deucrictibant to have
    injectable-like efficacy levels and a better tolerability profile than Orladeyo.
    Based on regulatory timelines discussed by PHVS, deucrictibant is unlikely to be on the market
    until 1H28, providing Orladeyo with a near 2yr runway as the sole oral HAE prophylaxis on the
    market. Deucrictibant has the potential to be the first HAE product to be approved for both
    on-demand use (PDUFA is 4/23/27) and for prophylaxis. Unclear at this point if a dual role for
    the bradykinin B2 receptor antagonist is an advantage or disadvantage in terms of efficacy/
    tolerability.                                                                   
    Besides quarterly sales update, the next significant catalyst for BCRX will likely be the readout
    of the ph 3 ALPHA-ORBIT trial evaluating navenibart as a long-acting (Q3M-Q6M dosing)
    injectable HAE prophylactic in 3Q27. We believe navenibart remains well positioned to offer a
    distinct dosing convenience value proposition for HAE patients prioritizing prophylaxis with
    high efficacy and very infrequent administration.

    through wk-24. Responder analyses were similarly strong, with 98.2%, 96.4%, and 65.5% of treated pts

    QoL Improved Meaningfully; Manageable Safety Profile. Deucrictibant demonstrated a 32.2 pt
    improvement from baseline in AE-QoL at wk-24 vs. 8.7 pts for pbo, representing a 23.5 pt pbo-adj
    benefit (p<0.0001). TEAEs occurred in 76.4% of deucrictibant pts vs. 56.7% on pbo, with most Grade
    1/2. Three Grade 3 and one Grade 4 events occurred on deucrictibant, with one SAE, one treatment
    discontinuation, and two Grade 2 liver-enzyme elevations, both with potential confounding factors.
    Prophy NDA Planned for 1H27; On-Demand PDUFA in April 2027. Deucrictibant IR has a PDUFA
    of 4/23/27 for FDA approval as an on-demand treatment. PHVS is targeting 1H27 NDA filing (likely
    post approval for on-demand treatment) for a prophylaxis usage of deucrictibant XR, with the filing
    expected to incorporate reusults from CHAPTER-3 and addl safety follow-up from the ongoing
    CHAPTER-4 OLE. The ph 3 CREAATE Part 1 topline data (from AAE-C1INH pts) are expected in 1Q27
    and could also be included in the filing depending on timing, though PHVS does not view CREAATE
    as gating the prophylaxis filing.

    VALUATION (PRICE TARGET: 16.00)                                                 
    Our Buy rating and 12-month price target of $16 are based on the average of two valuation methods: 1) a DCF analysis out to 2030,
    discounted at 10% with a 2% terminal growth rate; and 2) a multiples-based valuation method utilizing discounted (also 10%) 2030
    earnings, EBITDA, and sales estimates. The average of the two methods arrive at a valuation of $16/share.
