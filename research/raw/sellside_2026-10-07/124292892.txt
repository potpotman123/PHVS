     Wolfe on Data. Our initial impression is that the magnitude and consistency of 
     efficacy look very strong for an oral prophylactic. Results exceeded our expectations
     for success (~70%-80% ARR, initiation) and seems to be on par with Takzyro (87%
     ΔARR; 44% AFR) and far better than oral Orladeyo (44% ΔARR). The placebo-      
     adjusted reduction is strongly supported by the responder distribution, where nearly
     every treated patient achieved ≥70% reduction and almost half were entirely attack-
     free. On HAE-nC1INH patients, efficacy signal was highly variable and based on 
     only three treated patients, making it hard to comment on effects beyond Type 1/2.
     Management agrees that results should read through positively in Ph3 CREAATE for
     acquired C1-inhibitor deficiency due to clinical similarities to Type 1/2 HAE.

     Wolfe Research, LLC Fundamental Valuation Methodology:                         
     Company:               Fundamental Valuation Methodology:                      
     Pharvaris N.V.         Biotech Valuation Methodology. Our team uses a three-level approach when
                            valuing & rating biotech stocks: fundamental, narrative, positioning. Please reach
                            out for a fuller explanation of our methodology.        
                            Fundamental. Key inputs on this level are catalysts, earnings, and valuation.
                                                                                    
                            Biotech investors trade on peak revenue expectation on a vague and distant horizon.
                            Even inaccurate models may not be proven wrong until many years in the future,
                            esp pre-revenue biotechs. Therefore, to the extent that a stock is detached from
                            earnings, we choose to (i) lean more on R&D catalysts (ii) adopt certain buyside
                            assumptions if we see evidence of persistence. On mature biotechs, we value
                            catalysts vs earnings based on narrative weight. We prioritize P/E and EV/peak
                            sales over DCF because the former two methods appropriately include narrative and
                            positioning.

                            mature biotechs, to an extent), how the story is told has an outsized impact on how
                            stocks trade. This includes how attractive the biotech sector is due to policies, foreign
                            competition & M&A trends (i.e. sector-wide, macronarrative) and how individual
