                              Pharvaris Reports Phase III Results For Deucrictibant In HAE Prophylaxis
                              Pharvaris conducted a double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase III CHAPTER-3 study in HAE Type
                              1/2 and HAE Type 3 patients. The study enrolled N=85 patients, with 2:1 randomization across
                              40mg QD deucrictibant XR dosing arm and placebo. Primary endpoint of time-normalized
                              monthly attack rate was measured following the 24 week treatment period, with patients later
                              progressing to an OLE period.

                              We Believe Valuation Already Reflects Worst-Case Scenario Entry Of Competitive Oral Agent
                                                                                    
                              Understandably, investors worry that additional prophylactic entrants could significantly impact
                              Orladeyo share. However, our KOLs believe that well-controlled Orladeyo patients are unlikely
                              to switch to new oral/injectable entrants, as they already experience injectable-like efficacy
                              with their preference for an oral option. Our consultants expect a ~30-40%/60-70% split in orals/
                              injectable use. We expect Orladeyo will have a significant first mover advantage - Pharvaris
                              reported topline data from the Phase III study in September 2026 with study ongoing to capture
                              one year safety data which suggests fastest approval/launch timelines of 2028.
                                                                                    
                              Interestingly, on Pharvaris' Phase III data call the KOL reiterated that "the responses are variable
                              to Orladeyo" but "...there are patients doing extremely well on Orladeyo and I think they would
                              probably have little motivation to switch". This is a sentiment shared by our consultants, as they
                              believe Orladeyo is performing better in the real world. We have high conviction that Pharvaris
                              is unlikely to impact Orladeyo's already captured patient base. Stated more clearly, there would
                              be no reason for Orladeyo patients (beginning in 2028/2029) to switch. That would mean
                              deucrictibant would only impact new patients. Specifically, the long-term retention rate for
                              Orladeyo is very stable at ~60%, which sets a solid patient foundation.
                              Orladeyo Launch Is Progressing Nicely                 
                                                                                    
                              The company has indicated that the long-term patient retention at 12-months is roughly ~60%+,
                              and is largely maintained beyond 12-months, with the falloff ~1-2% post one year. Encouragingly,
                              the monthly discontinuation rate continued to decrease in 2025, as clinicians/patients gain
                              comfort with the Orladeyo administration profile. In August 2026, BioCryst indicated steady
                              quarterly patient adds, which suggests the new injectable product launches (CSL's Andembry
                              and Ionis' Dawnzera) are not impacting the oral Orladeyo segment. BioCryst will switch to
                              CareMed as the specialty pharmacy to fulfill shipment, which management hopes should help
                              scale access/fulfillment. Pediatric Orladeyo Rx are already being filled via CareMed, with Rx
                              for patients 12 years and older (Orladeyo capsules) to transition later in August. Management
                              expects no long-term impact to Orladeyo growth/sales from the transition.
                              Regarding clinician engagement, management indicates strong addition of new prescribers,
                              with increased penetration of the top 600 prescribers (that treat 50% of the U.S. HAE market).

                              Positive Interim Data From APeX-P Pediatric HAE Trial - August 2026 Launch
                              On December 12th, 2025 (PDUFA action date), BioCryst announced the FDA approval of
                              Orladeyo (oral pellets) for HAE patients ages 2-12 years old, with launch initiated in August 2026
                              with the same prescriber targets as Orladeyo tablet (for adults). There is an unmet need for
                              Orladeyo in this population, with a near-term opportunity in the ~500 diagnosed pediatric HAE
                              patients. Notably, BioCryst has indicated that ~40% are treated, with the potential to capture
                              both switch and new patients. In August, management highlighted 47 peds Rx (half completed
                              PAs with high approval rate) with pellet shipment in later in the month. We also expect pediatric
                              expansion will further drive overall patient demand for Orladeyo, to further entrench BioCryst as
                              KOLs indicate well-controlled patients are unlikely to switch.
                              As for early metrics, BioCryst indicated 47 prescriptions YTD which is tracking ahead of the
                              company's expectations for 2026. 50%+ of the Rx have completed prior authorization process
                              with a high approval rate.                            
                                                                                    
                              In February 2025, BioCryst reported positive interim results from the pediatric trial for Orladeyo
                              in HAE (APeX-P). The APeX-P trial was designed to inform weight based dosing of granule
                              formulation of Orladeyo for pediatric HAE patients. The trial includes an initial 12-week
                              standard-of-care treatment period, followed by open-label Orladeyo treatment period lasting up
                              to 144 weeks. The reported results include data from n=17 patients who had completed at least
                              48 weeks of Orladeyo treatment. A total of 29 patients have enrolled in the study.
                                                                                    
                              Treatment with Orladeyo led to sustained monthly attack rate reduction. During the standard of
                              care treatment period, the median monthly attack rate was 0.96, and mean monthly attack rate
                              was 1.5. After one month of Orladeyo treatment, median monthly attack rate was reduced to
                              0, while mean monthly attack rate was reduced to 0.5. Through one year of treatment, median
                              monthly attack rate was maintained, while mean monthly attack rate reduced to 0.3.
                              As for safety, Orladeyo showed a consistent AE profile to the adult and adolescent population
                              and AEs were similar across all ages and weights. Tolerability was also favorable, with 25
                              patients (out of 29 total) having continued on Orladeyo.

                              VALUATION METHODOLOGY AND RISKS                       
                                                                                    
                              Valuation Methodology                                 
                              Biotechnology:                                        
                              In calculating our 12-month target price, we employ one or more valuation methodologies,
                              which include a discounted earnings analysis, discounted cash flow analysis, net present value
                              analysis and/or a comparable company analysis. These analyses may or may not require
                              the use of objective measures such as price-to-earnings or price-to-sales multiples as well as
                              subjective measures such as discount rates.           
                              We make investment recommendations on certain early stage, pre-revenue companies based
                              upon an assessment of their business model, technology, probability of market success,
                              and the potential market opportunity, balanced by an assessment of applicable risks. Such
                              companies may not be assigned a price target.         
                                                                                    
                              Investment Risks                                      
                              Biotechnology:                                        
                                                                                    
                              There are multiple risks that are inherent with an investment in the biotechnology sector.
                              Beyond systemic risk, there is also clinical, regulatory, and commercial risk. Additionally,
