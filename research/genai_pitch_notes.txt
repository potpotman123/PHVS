OVERALL FRAMING
The investor drove the questions and challenged the assumptions. GenAI assisted
with retrieval, cross-document synthesis, data extraction, model implementation
and checks. Do not claim any calculation was impossible without AI. The defensible
benefit is the breadth and iteration of a traceable workflow; time saved is unmeasured.
Interface: Codex desktop. System identifies GPT-6 family; exact version/settings
and historical configuration not verified. Do not invent those for a methods slide.

EXAMPLE1 — CHALLENGING THE INITIAL CLINICAL SHORT (log G01/G02)
Objective: Determine whether missing paired observations and placebo differences
undermine efficacy, before assuming the commercial differentiation is real.
GenAI Method & Data Source: User-originated questions drove comparisons of registry,
CTIS, FDA and publication evidence; aggregate arithmetic and document-rule checks.
Snapshot of GenAI Output:134 randomized reconciles to88 paired +25 single-attack
+21 without a treated attack. FDA's sebetralstat placebo median was not estimable
under revised censoring, unlike the commonly quoted publication median.
Interpretation & Relevance to Thesis: Initial bearish interpretations weakened.
The useful outcome was discarding weak arguments, not manufacturing a short.
Limitations & Bias Mitigation: Patient-level causal adjustment unavailable; do not
claim superiority or absence of bias. Include the assistant's earlier oversimplified
placebo comparison and its correction. Same primary source repeated is not independent
corroboration. Evidence locations are in the log and current research state.

EXAMPLE3 — REVERSE VALUATION AS AN EXPECTATIONS TEST (G04/G05/G09)
Objective: Translate clinical/commercial arguments into required reimbursed sales.
GenAI Method & Data Source: User proposed separate IR/XR SOTP and reverse DCF, supplied
sell-side files, and challenged the IR-heavy result. AI audited source consistency,
implemented formulas, independently recalculated cash flows and retrieved launch data.
Snapshot of GenAI Output: Larger IR contribution follows an inherited62/38 source mix,
not an independent TAM conclusion. PHVS IR first full-calendar128m compares with an
Ekterly Q1annualized157m benchmark, not a verified matched first-year actual.
Interpretation & Relevance to Thesis: Exposes what is assumed versus implied; suggests
early IR ramp alone is insufficient evidence of excessive optimism. Later ramp,
net price, spending, adoption and product mix still require underwriting.
Limitations & Bias Mitigation: Single price does not uniquely identify two ramps;
PoS, timing, fixed mix and source probability basis are conditional. Arithmetic tests
and scenario checks do not independently validate demand or identify buy-side beliefs.

PRESENTATION GUARDRAILS / NEXT CAPTURE
Use a short user prompt beside an output snapshot and a specific resulting decision.
Show at least one disconfirming result and one corrected assistant assumption.
Do not present expert interviews as representative surveys or hypotheses as findings.
No systematic multi-prompt replication experiment has been run; repeated conversational
challenges are not such an experiment. If later performed, log prompts, source freeze,
outputs, disagreements and resolution rather than reporting only agreement.
Maintain source dates and label retrospective versus contemporaneous records.
Refresh model snapshots and thesis wording before building the deck; current numbers
are dated scenarios, not live-market claims. Keep licensed source excerpts minimal.

EXAMPLE5 — TURNING THE USER’S ADOPTION THESIS INTO AUDITABLE ECONOMICS (G59)
Objective: Defend or challenge the standalone short; treat acquisition separately.
GenAI Method & Data Source: User’s modeling choices guided public filing/claims
checks and an independent monthly cohort, financial and valuation engine.
Snapshot of GenAI Output: Working expected12month value$21.50 versus$56.27 in a
faster-adoption challenge; approximately$336m additional net funding in the
working both-success plan. Neither case includes a takeover premium.
Interpretation & Relevance to Thesis: Identifies capture, durable paid use and
cost/funding as the core claims to defend. Connecting persistence with PDC
exposed an implicit perfect-refill assumption; addressing it reduced target
value by about$1.52 while preserving the user’s efficacy-retention allowance.
Limitations & Bias Mitigation: No assumption range is a statistical interval.
Stronger-adoption and access scenarios test the short; they are not empirical
probabilities. Public sources replace pivotal licensed dependencies. Mechanical
checks verify arithmetic, not commercial accuracy. Exact settings and time
saved are unknown. The user explicitly reaffirmed the standalone short framing.
