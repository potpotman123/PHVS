GERD Background
H. pylori is a bacteria that damages the stomach and small intestine causing inflammation and pain.

GERD occurs when such acid flows into the esophagus on a repeated basis
Symptoms include heartburn, chest pain, etc.
Can be significant detriment to QoL and often includes cancer / ulcer complications

functional heartburn
